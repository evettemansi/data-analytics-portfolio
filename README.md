## Featured Projects

### 1. [Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard](Projects/Retail-Sales-Dashboard)

- Analyzed **$2.56M in sales** and approximately **$638K in profit**.
- Cleaned and transformed sales data with Power Query.
- Built a star-schema data model and DAX measures for sales, profit, margin, order count, and average order value.
- Evaluated performance by category, subcategory, region, channel, customer segment, and customer.
- Created an interactive Power BI executive dashboard with KPI cards and business insights.

**Tools:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Excel

### 2. [HR Workforce Analytics Dashboard](Projects/HR-Analytics-Dashboard)

- Analyzed a synthetic workforce dataset containing **2,400 employee records**.
- Cleaned and validated employee, department, and job-role data with Power Query.
- Built a relational model with a dedicated Date table and active/inactive date relationships.
- Developed DAX measures for headcount, hires, exits, workforce change, attrition, salary, and tenure.
- Created four report pages covering executive KPIs, attrition and retention, compensation and tenure, and workforce composition.
- Identified **1,719 active employees**, **681 exits**, an average active salary of **$79,310**, and an average active tenure of **2.7 years**.

**Tools:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Excel
